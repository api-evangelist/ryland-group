# Ryland Group

The Ryland Group was one of the largest U.S. homebuilders and a leading mortgage finance company before merging with Standard Pacific Corp in October 2015 to form CalAtlantic Group, Inc., which was subsequently acquired by Lennar Corporation in February 2018. During its operation, Ryland Homes built homes across 17 states and provided financial services including consumer mortgage loans, title, and escrow services. The company's technology stack included Akamai, Amazon CloudFront, ASP.NET, Fiserv for financial services, and Microsoft Dynamics for CRM and ERP.
